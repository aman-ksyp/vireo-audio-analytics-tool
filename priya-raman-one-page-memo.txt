EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The analysis covers the supplied Vireo support-ticket data and uses the stated reporting window of January 2025 through June 2026 where applicable.

Chat Frontline has the highest raw ticket volume: 3,030 tickets, representing 25.7% of the analyzed workload, with 40 agents and approximately 76 tickets per agent. Under the brief's stated hiring rule, the next two hires should therefore be assigned to Chat Frontline.

A simple capacity scenario shows tickets per agent moving from 75.75 with 40 agents to 72.14 with 42 agents, a reduction of approximately 4.8%. This is a capacity scenario, not a guaranteed improvement in productivity, SLA performance, or customer experience.

KEY BUSINESS FINDINGS

• Delivery & logistics is the largest displayed ticket category in the analytics view.
• Billing also represents a significant workload. Some Billing → Logistics handoffs may indicate opportunities to improve routing, but the current data does not establish causality.
• The dataset contains 11,780 tickets and 44 support agents overall.
• Observed refund value is approximately ₹5.33M across 1,894 tickets containing refund amounts. This is observed refund value, not estimated avoidable cost.
• The source data has inconsistent date coverage: ticket records extend earlier than the requested January 2025–June 2026 reporting window. Client-facing reporting should therefore retain the reporting-window caveat.

BUSINESS GOAL AND ₹ IMPACT

A practical business goal is to reduce policy-linked support leakage while maintaining service quality.

The support policy specifies:
• ₹350 store credit for every first-response SLA breach.
• ₹305 cost per internal transfer/re-handling event.

For Chat Frontline during the analyzed January 2025–June 2026 period:
• 3,030 tickets
• 226 first-response SLA breaches
• ₹79,100 SLA-credit exposure
• 245 internal transfers
• ₹74,725 transfer/re-handling cost
• Combined observed policy-linked exposure: ₹153,825

A 20% reduction scenario would represent approximately ₹30,765 of potential cost avoidance over the observed period.

This ₹30,765 figure is a scenario/target, not measured savings or a guaranteed financial result. It should be validated through subsequent operational changes and monitoring.

MONDAY HANDOFF

1. Assign the next two hires to Chat Frontline under the stated highest-volume-team rule.
2. Monitor monthly ticket volume by category and team.
3. Track first-response SLA breaches and internal transfers as operational cost signals.
4. Review low-confidence AI classifications before using them for operational decisions.
5. Investigate recurring Billing → Logistics handoffs to determine whether routing or ownership can be improved.
6. Recheck date coverage before using the dashboard for longer-term trend comparisons.
7. Treat the two-hire calculation as a capacity scenario and validate the effect after implementation.
